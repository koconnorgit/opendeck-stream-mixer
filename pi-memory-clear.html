Clear Preset
Clearing takes two taps. Press this key to arm it: it turns red and reads "Tap the button to clear it", and every Volume Memory key that holds a preset turns red too. Then tap the memory key you want to erase. Its stored volumes are removed and its label is kept. Press this key again, or wait 10 seconds, to cancel without clearing anything. Hold this key for 3 seconds to erase every preset on the deck at once.
